# Privacy policy

Vencord Italiano does not add telemetry or advertising.

The software may communicate with network services for functionality requested or enabled by the user:

- GitHub is contacted to check for and download Vencord Italiano releases and updates.
- Optional upstream Vencord cloud or synchronization features may contact the services configured by those features when the user enables them.
- RealVoiceTTS uses the speech-synthesis facilities exposed by Discord/Chromium and the operating system. Depending on the selected voice and operating-system configuration, a voice provider may use an online speech service.

No project-specific analytics service is operated by Vencord Italiano.

Users who do not want automatic release checks can disable automatic updates in Vencord settings.
